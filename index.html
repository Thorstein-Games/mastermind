How to play Mastermind

Mastermind is a classic code-breaking puzzle. The computer hides a secret code of 4 coloured pegs, each red, yellow, green or blue. Colours can repeat, so blue blue green blue is a valid code. You have 10 guesses to crack it.

After every guess, pins next to it tell you how close you are:

  • Black pin: one of your pegs is the right colour in the right spot.
  • White pin: one of your pegs is the right colour, but in the wrong spot.

The pins come in no particular order, so they don't say which peg they mean, and each secret peg is counted once. Use what each guess rules out to narrow down the code.

Mastermind FAQ

When is there a new code?

Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same daily code, so you can compare scores.

How do I share my result?

When you finish, tap Share result. Like Wordle, it shares only your pins, never the colours, so it won't spoil the code for anyone else.

How do I challenge a friend?

Tap the ✉️ button, set your own secret code, pick 10 to 15 guesses and send the link. The code is scrambled in the link, so your friend can't peek at it.

Is it free?

Yes. Play free in any browser on your phone, tablet or computer. No download or account needed.
